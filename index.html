ClipStack

A private clipboard history for iPhone and iPad. Encrypted, on-device, and gone when you want it gone.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, and choose ClipStack.
  2. Tap ClipStack and turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard types without it, but iOS requires it for any keyboard to read the clipboard. ClipStack has no network code.
  3. Copy some text, then switch to ClipStack with the globe key. Tap an item to type it.

Add the Control Center button

Open Control Center, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control, and choose ClipStack. You can also assign it in Settings → Action Button → Controls.

Common questions

Why does ClipStack ask for Full Access?

iOS gives keyboards access to the clipboard and to data shared with their app only with Full Access. ClipStack uses it for nothing else, contains no networking code, and stores your history encrypted on the device.

Why didn't something I copied get saved?

ClipStack skips passwords, one-time codes, card numbers and API keys by default. You can change this in Settings → Never save. Items also delete themselves after the time set in Settings → Auto-delete after.

Does my history sync between devices?

No. History never leaves the device it was copied on and isn't included in backups.

How do the trial and purchase work?

The 7-Day Trial is free and never charges or renews. After it ends, a one-time purchase unlocks ClipStack forever on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases in Settings if you reinstall.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Erase everything now, or tap the trash key twice in the keyboard. This deletes the history and destroys its encryption key.
