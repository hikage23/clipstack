ClipStack Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version

ClipStack has no servers. Nothing you copy or type is ever sent to us or to anyone else. We couldn't receive it even if we wanted to: the app contains no networking code, and every build is automatically audited to prove it. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party code. A few things are handled by iOS itself, as they are for every app; they're listed below so nothing is a surprise.

What ClipStack saves

Exactly two things, both only on this device.

  1. Your clipboard history. Text you copy is saved only when you open the ClipStack keyboard or tap Save clipboard. It's encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key kept in this device's Keychain that never syncs or leaves the device. History never syncs to iCloud or your other devices, is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, and deletes itself after the time you choose in Settings. Erase everything now deletes it and destroys its key. Deleting the app deletes it too.
  2. Your settings, such as auto-delete times and keyboard options. They never contain anything you copied or typed.

What ClipStack never saves or sends

Your keystrokes. The text around your cursor, which the keyboard reads only in memory to capitalize, correct and suggest words, then discards. Anything a password manager marks as concealed, and by default card numbers, one-time codes, passwords and API keys. Usage data, identifiers or any information about you. Items you copy from ClipStack never go to Universal Clipboard on your other devices.

What iOS does, outside ClipStack

These are handled by iOS the same way they are for every app and for Apple's own keyboard. ClipStack doesn't control them, and none of them include your clipboard history.

  • Words you keep. When you undo an autocorrection or tap your exact spelling in the suggestion bar, ClipStack asks iOS's spell checker to remember that word so it stops correcting it, just like Apple's keyboard does. iOS stores the word on this device, and it may be included in your device backup. To clear these words, go to Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › Reset › Reset Keyboard Dictionary.
  • Settings in backups. Your ClipStack settings are included in your iCloud or computer backup, like any app's preferences. They contain no content.
  • Purchases. The free trial and one-time unlock go through Apple's App Store. Buying or restoring connects your device to Apple, never to us, and nothing you copy or type is included. ClipStack checks Apple's signed receipt on your device and never receives your name, email or payment details.
  • Crash reports. Only if you've turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us a report of where the app crashed. ClipStack never writes clipboard content or typing into logs or crash messages.
  • Secure fields. In password and phone number fields, iOS switches to Apple's keyboard. ClipStack never sees what you type there.

Keyboard and Full Access

The keyboard types without Full Access. Allowing Full Access only lets it read the clipboard, play key sounds and haptics, and open the encrypted history it shares with the app on this device. iOS's switch would also allow network access, but ClipStack contains no networking code to use it.

Children

ClipStack collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
