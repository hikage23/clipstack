ClipStack Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version

ClipStack doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party code. The app contains no networking code at all, and every build is automatically audited to prove it.

What stays on your device

Text you copy is saved only when you open the ClipStack keyboard or tap Save clipboard. It's encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key lives in this device's Keychain and never syncs or leaves the device. History is excluded from iCloud and device backups, and it deletes itself automatically after the time you choose in Settings. Erase everything now deletes the history and destroys its key. Deleting the app also deletes your history.

What is never saved

Anything a password manager marks as concealed, and by default card numbers, one-time codes, passwords and API keys. ClipStack never records what you type with its keyboard.

Keyboard and Full Access

The keyboard types without Full Access. Allowing Full Access only lets it read the clipboard and the encrypted history shared with the app. It has no network access to use, and it never sends anything anywhere.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. ClipStack checks Apple's signed receipt on your device and never receives your name, email or payment details.

Children

ClipStack collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
